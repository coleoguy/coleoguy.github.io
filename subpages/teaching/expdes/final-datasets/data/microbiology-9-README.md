# Yogurt texture across retail lots

Data file: `microbiology-9.csv`

One unopened cup of plain cultured dairy yogurt was sampled per independent retail lot. Label category was recorded rather than assigned; no culturing was performed.

Simulated data; 74 observations. No real people, specimens, or institutions are represented.

## Variables

- `firmness_n`: Force at a standardized probe depth (N). Continuous response.
- `label_style`: Straining category on the container. Categorical predictor. Levels: `strained`, `unstrained`.
- `container_width_cm`: External container width (cm). Continuous predictor.
- `protein_g_100g`: Labelled protein per 100 g of yogurt (g). Continuous predictor.
- `test_temp_c`: Product temperature at measurement (degrees C). Continuous predictor.
