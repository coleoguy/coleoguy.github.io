# Submerged plants in isolated ponds

Data file: `ecology-3.csv`

One row represents an independently sampled pond. Surrounding land cover was recorded rather than assigned; the response is the average of subsamples within a pond.

Simulated data; 112 observations. No real people, specimens, or institutions are represented.

## Variables

- `plant_biomass_g_m2`: Mean submerged plant dry mass per square meter (g per square m). Continuous response.
- `water_ph`: Water pH on collection day. Continuous predictor.
- `water_clarity_cm`: Water transparency measured as Secchi depth (cm). Continuous predictor.
- `land_cover`: Predominant land cover immediately around the pond. Categorical predictor. Levels: `grassland`, `woodland`.
- `shoreline_slope_deg`: Mean slope of the immediate shore (degrees). Continuous predictor.
