# Egg mass variation across flock housing types

Data file: `poultry-science-3.csv`

Each row represents one simulated flock sampled once, with one flock per farm. Housing format and flock characteristics were observed rather than assigned.

Simulated data; 69 observations. No real people, specimens, or institutions are represented.

## Variables

- `mean_egg_mass_g`: Flock mean egg mass in grams. Continuous response.
- `flock_age_weeks`: Age of the flock at sampling in weeks. Continuous predictor.
- `sampling_window_min`: Duration of the egg sampling window in minutes. Continuous predictor.
- `housing_format`: Housing format observed at the farm. Categorical predictor. Levels: `aviary`, `floor_pen`.
- `nest_lip_height_cm`: Measured height of the nest-box lip in centimeters. Continuous predictor.
