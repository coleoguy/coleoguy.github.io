# Lamb conformation under routine management

Data file: `animal-breeding-1.csv`

One row represents one lamb from a different dam in a simulated managed flock. Breed cross was recorded, and all measurements were taken at the same age.

Simulated data; 108 observations. No real people, specimens, or institutions are represented.

## Variables

- `hip_height_cm`: Height at the hip (cm). Continuous response.
- `dam_age_years`: Age of the dam at lambing (years). Continuous predictor.
- `birth_mass_kg`: Recorded body mass at birth (kg). Continuous predictor.
- `breed_cross`: Recorded genetic cross category. Categorical predictor. Levels: `cross_a`, `cross_b`, `cross_c`.
- `mean_pen_temp_c`: Mean pen temperature during the rearing interval (degrees C). Continuous predictor.
