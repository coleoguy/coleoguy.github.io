# Postharvest firmness of greenhouse strawberries

Data file: `horticulture-1.csv`

Each row is one fruit picked from a different greenhouse plant. Plants were randomly assigned a potting medium; fruit and its surroundings were measured at harvest.

Simulated data; 68 observations. No real people, specimens, or institutions are represented.

## Variables

- `firmness_n`: Firmness measured as peak compression force in newtons. Continuous response.
- `fruit_width_mm`: Fruit width in millimeters. Continuous predictor.
- `substrate`: Assigned potting medium. Categorical predictor. Levels: `coir`, `peat_mix`.
- `pedicel_length_mm`: Length of the fruit stalk in millimeters. Continuous predictor.
- `soluble_solids_brix`: Soluble solids in degrees Brix. Continuous predictor.
