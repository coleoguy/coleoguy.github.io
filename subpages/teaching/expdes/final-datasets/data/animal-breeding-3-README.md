# Fleece quality across sheep lines

Data file: `animal-breeding-3.csv`

Each row describes one adult sheep from a different maternal family in a simulated managed flock. Breed line was recorded rather than assigned, and all animals were sampled in the same season.

Simulated data; 97 observations. No real people, specimens, or institutions are represented.

## Variables

- `fiber_diameter_um`: Mean diameter of measured wool fibers (micrometers). Continuous response.
- `room_humidity_pct`: Relative humidity during fiber measurement (percent). Continuous predictor.
- `breed_line`: Recorded breed line. Categorical predictor. Levels: `line_a`, `line_b`.
- `body_mass_kg`: Body mass at shearing (kg). Continuous predictor.
- `staple_length_mm`: Unstretched wool staple length (mm). Continuous predictor.
