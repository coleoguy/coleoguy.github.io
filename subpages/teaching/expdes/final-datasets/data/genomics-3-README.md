# Genome-size estimates in fern accessions

Data file: `genomics-3.csv`

One accession represented each independently collected fern population. Ploidy category was assessed separately and was not assigned.

Simulated data; 72 observations. No real people, specimens, or institutions are represented.

## Variables

- `genome_pg`: Estimated DNA content of a haploid genome (pg). Continuous response.
- `ploidy_class`: Cytologically assessed ploidy class. Categorical predictor. Levels: `diploid`, `tetraploid`.
- `collection_elev_m`: Elevation at collection (m). Continuous predictor.
- `spore_area_um2`: Mean projected spore area (square micrometers). Continuous predictor.
- `frond_water_pct`: Water as fraction of fresh frond mass (percent). Continuous predictor.
