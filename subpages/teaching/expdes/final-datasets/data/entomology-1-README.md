# Walking speed in flour beetles

Data file: `entomology-1.csv`

Each row is one adult flour beetle tested once in a clean arena. Independent beetles were randomly assigned to one texture; speed was summarized over a fixed observation window.

Simulated data; 86 observations. No real people, specimens, or institutions are represented.

## Variables

- `speed_mm_s`: Mean walking speed during the trial (mm/s). Continuous response.
- `room_humidity_pct`: Relative humidity at trial start (percent). Continuous predictor.
- `surface_texture`: Assigned arena surface. Categorical predictor. Levels: `smooth`, `fine_grit`, `coarse_grit`.
- `arena_illuminance_lux`: Illuminance at arena center (lux). Continuous predictor.
- `body_length_mm`: Adult body length (mm). Continuous predictor.
