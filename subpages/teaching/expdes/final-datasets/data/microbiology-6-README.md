# Kombucha label and dissolved solids

Data file: `microbiology-6.csv`

One sealed bottle from each independently purchased batch of retail nonalcoholic kombucha was sampled. Product styles were recorded and the products were not fermented in the study.

Simulated data; 66 observations. No real people, specimens, or institutions are represented.

## Variables

- `solids_brix`: Refractometer estimate of soluble solids (degrees Brix). Continuous response.
- `bottle_temp_c`: Bottle temperature during reading (degrees C). Continuous predictor.
- `fill_height_mm`: Liquid fill height within bottle (mm). Continuous predictor.
- `style`: Labelled flavor style. Categorical predictor. Levels: `plain`, `fruit`, `herbal`.
- `label_sugar_g`: Labelled total sugar per 100 mL (g). Continuous predictor.
