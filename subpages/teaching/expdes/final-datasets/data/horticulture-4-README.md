# Marketable leaf mass in greenhouse kale

Data file: `horticulture-4.csv`

Each row represents one separately potted kale plant. Mulch was randomly assigned and leafy biomass was measured once per plant.

Simulated data; 75 observations. No real people, specimens, or institutions are represented.

## Variables

- `marketable_mass_g`: Fresh mass of intact marketable leaves in grams. Continuous response.
- `light_mol_m2_d`: Mean daily light integral in moles of photosynthetically active photons per square meter per day. Continuous predictor.
- `initial_seedling_height_cm`: Seedling height at transplant in centimeters. Continuous predictor.
- `pot_rim_cm`: Pot opening diameter in centimeters. Continuous predictor.
- `mulch`: Mulch assigned to the pot. Categorical predictor. Levels: `paper`, `straw`, `none`.
