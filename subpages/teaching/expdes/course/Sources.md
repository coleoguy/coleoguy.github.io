# Sources and teaching scope

The course was rebuilt from the earlier [Experimental Design materials](https://coleoguy.github.io/subpages/expdes.html) in the [Blackmon Lab website repository](https://github.com/coleoguy/coleoguy.github.io). The source audit included 12 decks (395 slides), methods notes, continuous-data examples, and the 45-cell test picker. The older black title band, white heading, gray slide body, and code beside plots guided the design.

All worked datasets, plots, analyses, and interpretations in this collection are new simulations. Methods are grouped once and linked from all relevant picker cells. Welch ANOVA and an explicit pooled-t comparison strengthen the original chooser. Bayesian inference is outside this frequentist collection. PCA, MDS, LDA, resampling, and interval estimation retain their proper roles rather than being mislabeled as tests.
