# Odonata

What determines rates of chromosome evolution in Odonata

<!-- tealc:project-status-start -->

**Status:** active

**Current hypothesis:**

Chromosome number evolution in Odonata is dominated by Robertsonian fusions occurring faster than fissions, producing a directional bias toward chromosome number reduction, and sex-autosome fusions occur at rates consistent with or slightly exceeding neutral random expectation, suggesting possible selective advantage for incorporating autosomes into the sex chromosome system.
