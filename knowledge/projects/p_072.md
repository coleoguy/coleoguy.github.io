# Full tree of life sex chromosome evolution

Full tree-of-life comparative analysis of sex chromosome system evolution, integrating the Fragile Y hypothesis with phylogenetic models of XY/ZW/XO/ZO transitions across eukaryotes.

<!-- tealc:project-status-start -->

**Status:** active

**Current hypothesis:**

Recurrent selection to reduce X-Y recombination shrinks the pseudoautosomal region, increasing aneuploidy risk and driving Y chromosome loss — the Fragile Y hypothesis — and this dynamic explains the phylogenetic distribution of sex chromosome systems across the eukaryotic tree of life.
