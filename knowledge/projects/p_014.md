# Astyanax Starvation

how does genome respond to starvation in cave vs surface fish.

<!-- tealc:project-status-start -->

**Status:** active

**Current hypothesis:**

DNA methylation in Astyanax mexicanus is primarily shaped by population-level evolutionary divergence rather than acute starvation responses, with cave and surface fish showing strongly differentiated methylation profiles across tissues, while starvation-induced transcriptional changes occur largely independently of CpG methylation remodeling.
