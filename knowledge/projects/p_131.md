# Dismantling chromosomal stasis manuscript (CURE-2025)

Flagship Nature/Science/Cell-tier paper. 63,682 karyotypes, 55 eukaryotic clades, 844-fold rate variation in dysploidy. Birds sit ABOVE global median once microchromosomes resolved.

**Status:** active

**Current hypothesis:**

Chromosome number stasis across the eukaryotic tree of life is a methodological artifact of inadequate sampling and insensitive comparative methods. With 63K+ karyotypes and modern dysploidy-rate inference, the apparent stasis dissolves into 844-fold rate variation across clades, with avian "stasis" reversing once microchromosomes are properly resolved.
