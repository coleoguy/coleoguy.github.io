# Compound Traits

<!-- tealc:project-status-start -->

**Status:** active

**Current hypothesis:**

Phenotypic scale choice fundamentally determines whether genetic effects appear additive or epistatic in line cross analysis, and appropriate transformations of compound traits can reveal underlying additive genetic architecture that would otherwise be obscured by apparent non-additive interactions. The central question is how to identify and apply optimal phenotypic transformations for compound traits so that generation means analysis accurately reflects true gene action.
