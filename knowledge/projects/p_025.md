**Status:** active

**Current hypothesis:**

The genetic architecture of aggression in Betta splendens can be characterized through latent class analysis (LCA) by crossing domesticated Plakat and wild-type individuals, revealing the number and nature of underlying genetic factors controlling aggressive behavior. Differences in aggression between domesticated and wild-type Betta reflect discrete genetic components that can be mapped and quantified using hybrid crosses.
