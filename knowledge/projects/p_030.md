# Chromosome Selection

is there a specific number of chromosomes that is most fit given levels and types of epistasis and environmental stability

<!-- tealc:project-status-start -->

**Status:** active

**Current hypothesis:**

The number of chromosomes an organism carries affects adaptive fitness in fluctuating environments because more chromosomes produce more obligate crossovers per meiosis, accelerating the breakdown of linkage disequilibrium and enabling faster reassembly of favorable multi-locus genotypes; this fitness advantage is predicted to be conditional on populations being variation-limited and modulated by the type and level of epistasis.
