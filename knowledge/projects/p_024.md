# Discrete Traits and Hybridization

how does hybridization effect application of PCMs for discrete traits

<!-- tealc:project-status-start -->

**Status:** active

**Current hypothesis:**

Hybridization and introgression violate the bifurcating tree assumption underlying phylogenetic comparative methods (PCMs), and the degree and type of hybridization will systematically bias inferences about discrete trait evolution, including ancestral state estimation, rate estimation, and model selection. Simulations using SLiM across varying hybridization histories will quantify the conditions under which standard PCMs fail or produce overconfident results.
