# Bombus Genomics

Population genomics of bumble bee species in sky islands

<!-- tealc:project-status-start -->

**Status:** active

**Current hypothesis:**

Bumble bee populations inhabiting isolated sky island mountain ranges experience reduced gene flow and genetic differentiation due to geographic isolation, with population genomic structure reflecting the degree of connectivity between mountain habitats. Two sympatric Bombus species may show differential responses to sky island fragmentation based on their ecology and dispersal ability.
