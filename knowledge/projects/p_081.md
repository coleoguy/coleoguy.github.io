# Compound traits and evolution

<!-- tealc:project-status-start -->

**Status:** active

**Current hypothesis:**

The scale on which a compound phenotypic trait is measured fundamentally determines whether genetic effects appear additive, dominant, or epistatic, such that appropriate transformations can reveal the true mode of gene action underlying trait variation. Line cross analysis can be used to identify optimal measurement scales that linearize genetic effects and distinguish genuine epistasis from scale-induced interaction artifacts.
