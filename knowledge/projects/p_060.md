# Tomatoes - Strains

<!-- tealc:project-status-start -->

**Status:** active

**Current hypothesis:**

Two tomato strains (P1 and P2) differ in fruit development time and other traits, and systematic reciprocal crosses, backcrosses, and selfing generations (F1, F2, BC1, BC2) will allow characterization of the genetic basis and inheritance mode of these differences, including potential maternal or cytoplasmic effects revealed by reciprocal cross comparisons.
