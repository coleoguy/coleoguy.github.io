# SC/SI Chromosome Number

Does self compatibility/incompatability impact rates of chromosome number evolution.

<!-- tealc:project-status-start -->

**Status:** active

**Current hypothesis:**

Self-compatibility versus self-incompatibility mating systems differentially influence the rates of chromosome number evolution, including whole-genome duplication (polyploidy), demi-polyploidy, and chromosomal fusion/fission events across angiosperm lineages. Specifically, the mating system (SC/SI) is predicted to alter selective pressures on chromosome number changes, leading to detectable differences in macroevolutionary transition rates.
