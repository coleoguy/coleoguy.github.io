# Genomes of Jewels

genomes of 3 remaining Chrysina species in north america

<!-- tealc:project-status-start -->

**Status:** active

**Current hypothesis:**

The X chromosome of Chrysina scarab beetles exhibits distinct evolutionary dynamics compared to autosomes, characterized by reduced transposable element accumulation, slower molecular evolution (Slower-X rather than Faster-X), and greater architectural constraint, while autosomes drive ecological divergence through lineage-specific positive selection and TE-mediated structural variation.
