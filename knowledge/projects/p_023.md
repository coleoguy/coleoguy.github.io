# Achiasmy Synthesis

review and synthesis of achiasmy across the tree of life.

<!-- tealc:project-status-start -->

**Status:** active

**Current hypothesis:**

Achiasmy (the absence of crossing over during meiosis) has evolved repeatedly across diverse lineages and its distribution across the tree of life follows patterns related to sex chromosome evolution and heterogamety. A synthesis of achiasmy occurrences will reveal common genomic, cytological, and evolutionary factors that predispose certain taxa or sex chromosomes to lose recombination.
