# Teaching-research integration model paper

A methods/perspective paper describing Heath's teaching-research integration model: Biology & AI CURE (51 students, 51 original projects, 33 posters April 2026), formal AI concentration stood up with the department, 58+ undergraduates trained with many authoring preprints and papers. Target venues: BioScience education section or PLoS Biology Education section. Paper is about the MODEL, not the biology the students produce.

<!-- tealc:project-status-start -->

**Status:** active

**Current hypothesis:**

A structured course-based undergraduate research experience (CURE) that pairs each student with an original AI-assisted biology project, embedded within a formal departmental concentration, produces measurably higher rates of student authorship and research independence than traditional lab-course models.

**Next action:**

Outline the paper: (1) model description, (2) outcome metrics (n students, n preprints/papers, concentration enrollment), (3) comparison to CURE literature, (4) replication requirements. Draft outline in Google Doc.
