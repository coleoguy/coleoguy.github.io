# PAR estimation

Computational estimation of pseudoautosomal region (PAR) size across diverse taxa. Primary author: Megan Copeland. Comparative analysis of PAR size and its relationship to sex chromosome divergence rates. Data in PAR/ and PAR_analysis/ folders.

<!-- tealc:project-status-start -->

**Status:** active

**Current hypothesis:**

Pseudoautosomal region (PAR) size can be estimated computationally from read-depth and recombination signals across diverse taxa, and PAR size variation predicts rates of sex chromosome divergence and Y chromosome loss consistent with the Fragile Y hypothesis.
