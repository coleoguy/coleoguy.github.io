# Fusion Probabilities

<!-- tealc:project-status-start -->

**Status:** active

**Current hypothesis:**

The probability of chromosomal fusion events varies predictably across taxa and can be modeled as a function of genomic or biological factors such as chromosome number, genome size, or life history traits. Understanding these fusion probabilities may explain karyotype evolution and diversification across lineages.
