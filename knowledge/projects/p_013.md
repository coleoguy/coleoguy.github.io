# SeqDef (DataViz)

a new statitistic that categorizes species on a phylogeny in their priority for sequencing based on current sequence availability and another trait of interest

<!-- tealc:project-status-start -->

**Status:** active

**Current hypothesis:**

A phylogeny-aware sequencing deficiency statistic can quantitatively prioritize species for genomic sequencing by weighting the need for new sequence data based on both the availability of existing sequence data and the phylogenetic relatedness of taxa, such that closely related species with available data reduce the sequencing priority of focal taxa.
