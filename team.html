Twenty scientists, one lab.

A lot of what we do here hinges on bringing undergraduates into fundamental research earlier than most labs do. Ten of the people listed below are undergraduate researchers running their own projects, often publishing before they graduate. The list that follows covers the principal investigator, research staff, graduate students, a post-bacc, undergraduate researchers, and alumni.

Principal investigator

Heath Blackmon
Associate Professor · Opened the lab in 2017
Heath earned his PhD in 2015 from the Demuth lab at UT Arlington, where he studied the evolution of sex chromosomes and karyotypes in Coleoptera. He then completed a postdoc at the University of Minnesota with Emma Goldberg and Yaniv Brandvain, and opened the Blackmon Lab at Texas A&M in the fall of 2017. He also chairs the TAMU EEB interdisciplinary doctoral program. Curriculum Vitae · Google Scholar · ORCID

Kaya Harper
Biology PhD · Joined 2023
Her research explores how environmental variation shapes genomic architecture, epigenetic regulation, and phenotypic plasticity across natural populations, and how evolutionary processes interact with human-driven environmental change to influence adaptation, resilience, and vulnerability under shifting climatic conditions. Outside the lab she runs, hikes, scuba dives, and explores the kinds of dynamic landscapes that inspire her work. Website
