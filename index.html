Evolution 1.0 → AI 1.1

Darwin gave biology its operating system in 1859. Evolution 1.0 — a framework so generative that nothing in our field has made sense without it for 165 years. AI 1.1 is the next instrument biologists pick up: a tool that lets us read the literature, integrate the data, and reason about life at scales no individual researcher ever could. Same discipline. Same questions. Vastly bigger reach.

We think this is the most exciting moment to be doing evolutionary biology in a generation, and we are working in the open — agent-readable data, validated pipelines, published failure modes — so the next set of researchers (and the AI systems they use) can pick up where we leave off.

Featured · April 2026 preprint

Dismantling Chromosomal Stasis Across the Eukaryotic Tree of Life

Copeland M, McConnell M, Barboza A, et al. — bioRxiv (2026)

63,682 karyotypes across 55 eukaryotic clades show dysploidy rates vary 844-fold, and that even birds — the textbook example of chromosomal stasis — exceed the global median once microchromosome dynamics are resolved. Stasis isn't kingdom-deep; it's life-history-deep.
