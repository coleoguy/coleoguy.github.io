Experimental · expected to fail

AI as lead investigator

We handed a complete research problem in claw evolution to a Claude-powered agent and asked it to run the project from hypothesis to manuscript. We expect it to fail, and the failure is the point. Each breakdown teaches us how to design the next version: an agent that works at our side as scientific collaborator, programmer, and an army of students and postdocs, doing meaningful work when the task is correctly scoped, tested, and examined.

April 2026 · bioRxiv

Dismantling chromosomal stasis across the eukaryotic tree of life

Copeland M, McConnell M, Barboza A, et al. · bioRxiv 2026

We looked at 63,682 karyotypes across 55 eukaryotic clades and found that dysploidy rates vary by a factor of 844. Even birds, often cited as the textbook example of chromosomal stasis, sit above the global median once microchromosome dynamics are accounted for. Chromosomal stasis tracks life history, not taxonomic group.
